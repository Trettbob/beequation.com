Play Beequation free online

Beequation is a mental maths puzzle game. Every honeycomb hides one number that two others add up to, or multiply to. Find it and tap it.

Choose Beequation Kids (ages 5 to 12, with Tally the bee, stars and stickers and no clock) or Beequation for teens and adults (a Daily Pro puzzle, a 60-second blitz and expert levels). It's free, there's no sign-up, and your progress is saved in this browser only.
