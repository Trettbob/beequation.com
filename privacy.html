Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Beequation and Beequation Kids ("the apps") are published by Trett Labs Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17310137), registered office The 1812 Building, Wheatley Park, Mirfield, West Yorkshire, WF14 8HE, United Kingdom ("we", "us").

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and send no personal data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Game progress (stars, stickers, scores, streaks and settings) and any player names and pictures you add are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Reminders and the grown-ups lock

If you turn on the daily reminder in Beequation, it is scheduled by your device itself. No notification service or device token is used, and nothing is sent to us. If a grown-up turns on the Face ID, fingerprint or passcode lock in Beequation Kids, the check is done by the device; the app only learns whether it passed and never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, payment details or contact information. Their privacy policies apply to the purchase.

Sharing (Beequation only)

If you choose to share a result, the app opens your device's own share sheet with a short text message. We do not see what you share or who you share it with.

Children

Beequation Kids is designed for children aged 5 to 12. We do not collect personal information from children, and the app contains no ads and no social features. Its only links out (to our help and privacy pages) are in the grown-up area. Purchases and settings sit behind a grown-up check. We design the apps in line with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can contact us with any question at hello@beequation.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the apps' store listings.

Contact: Trett Labs Limited, hello@beequation.com

This website

beequation.com uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. If you choose a language from the language menu, your browser remembers that choice on your device; nothing is sent to us. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
