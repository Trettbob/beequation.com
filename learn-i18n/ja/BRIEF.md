# Japan (ja): country brief for the Learn section

Written for the writers of `learn-i18n/ja/`. Read it before writing any page. Japanese terms are quoted in Japanese.
Anything not confirmed from an official text is marked **UNCERTAIN**: leave it out of the pages, or phrase it
loosely (「多くの学校では…」「教科書によって違いますが…」), rather than state it as a rule.

## 1. The school system

| Stage | Years (local names) | Ages | Compulsory? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 幼稚園 (MEXT), 保育所 / 保育園 (こども家庭庁), 認定こども園 | 「年少」「年中」「年長」 (3年保育); in 保育所 also 「3歳児クラス」「4歳児クラス」「5歳児クラス」 | 3 to 6 | No. Free for ages 3–5 since October 2019 (幼児教育・保育の無償化); almost all children attend one of the three |
| 小学校 | 1年生 to 6年生 (「小学1年生」「小1」…「小6」); 低学年 (1–2年), 中学年 (3–4年), 高学年 (5–6年) | 6 to 12 | Yes |
| 中学校 | 1年生 to 3年生 (「中1」–「中3」) | 12 to 15 | Yes |

- Compulsory schooling (義務教育) is **nine years**: 小学校 6 + 中学校 3 (学校教育法 第16条). A child starts 小学校 at "the beginning
  of the first school year after the day after they reach the age of six" (第17条). In practice a class is **all children born
  from 2 April to 1 April of the next year** (children born on 1 April go with the older year, because legally you reach an age
  the day before your birthday). Some 公立中高一貫校 and private schools take children from 中1 by exam (see 中学受験 in section 3).
- **The school year starts on 1 April** and ends on 31 March. 入学式 is in early April, 卒業式 in March. So a 1年生 is 6 at the
  start of the year and turns 7 during it: the year is described as 「6〜7歳」.
- Writing years: 「小学1年生」 in full, 「1年生」 in context, 「小1」 informally. Half-width digits on our pages (「小学1年生」),
  as on our site. Pre-school year: 「年長」 or 「年長さん」 (friendly), 「5歳児」 in official texts, 「就学前」 (the year before school).
- The ministry (文部科学省, MEXT) publishes one national curriculum for all schools: the **学習指導要領**. Unlike Spain or Germany
  there are no regional curricula; textbooks (教科書) are written by private publishers, approved by MEXT (教科書検定) and chosen by
  each municipality (採択). Every child gets them free. Six publishers produce primary 算数 books (東京書籍, 啓林館, 大日本図書,
  学校図書, 教育出版, 日本文教出版); new editions came in April 2024. **Don't name publishers on pages**; say 「教科書によって
  順番が少し違います」 where it matters.
- 算数 is taught by the class teacher (担任の先生) in most classes; many schools split large classes or use 少人数指導 /
  習熟度別指導 in 算数 (prevalence UNCERTAIN). From 2022, subject teachers (教科担任制) are spreading in 5–6年 for 算数, 理科,
  英語 and 体育 (scope UNCERTAIN; mention loosely).
- One device per child (GIGAスクール構想, since 2021): children use a tablet or Chromebook at school; many schools set 「ドリル」
  apps or online homework (which apps: UNCERTAIN, don't name).
- 「算数」 is the primary school subject; it becomes 「数学」 in 中学校. Our pages say 算数 throughout.

### Years our Learn section covers

The English section covers ages 4/5 to 11 (end of pre-school to end of primary). The Japanese one covers **年長 and
小学1年生 to 小学6年生** (ages 5 to 12): the last pre-school year and the whole of 小学校.

Japanese children are about 5 months older than English children in the same-numbered year, and one year "behind" by
number. Don't mention English Years or US grades on the Japanese pages.

### School calendar, reports, daily life

- Most schools use **3学期制**: 1学期 (April to about 20 July), 夏休み (late July to the end of August; some regions start
  earlier), 2学期 (September to about 25 December), 冬休み (to about 7 January), 3学期 (January to March), 春休み (late March to
  early April). Some municipalities use 2学期制 (前期・後期, split in October). Dates vary by municipality: on pages say
  「夏休み」「2学期」 rather than dates.
- Events that make good contexts: 入学式, 遠足, 運動会 (May–June or September–October), 授業参観, 個人面談 / 懇談会, 家庭訪問
  (some areas), 夏休みの宿題 (ドリル, 自由研究), 学芸会 / 音楽会, 持久走大会, 6年生の修学旅行, 卒業式.
- **Reports**: the official school record is the 指導要録; what families get is the school's own **通知表** (often called
  「あゆみ」「通信票」), usually at the end of each 学期. Each subject is assessed against three 観点:
  「知識・技能」「思考・判断・表現」「主体的に学習に取り組む態度」, typically as 「よくできる・できる・もう少し」 or ◎○△
  (wording is school-specific: UNCERTAIN). From 3年, an overall 評定 in three levels (3・2・1) is recorded; 1–2年 have
  観点 only (MEXT 通知, 2019). Pages: describe it loosely ("通知表の書き方は学校によって違います").
- Repeating a year (留年) does not happen in Japanese primary schools in practice: all children move up (年齢主義). Don't mention
  repeating.
- 算数 lessons: 1年 136 hours a year (about 4 a week), 2年–6年 175 hours a year (about 5 a week), 45 minutes each.
- After school: 学童保育 (放課後児童クラブ), 習い事 (スイミング, ピアノ, そろばん教室, 公文式-type 教室: **don't name brands**),
  塾 (cram school, especially for 中学受験).

## 2. What the curriculum expects, year by year

**How the official curriculum is built.** The 学習指導要領 gives 算数 content **per grade** (unlike Spain's ciclos), in four
domains (領域) that change at 4年:

- 1年–3年: **A 数と計算, B 図形, C 測定, D データの活用**
- 4年–6年: **A 数と計算, B 図形 (now including area, volume, angles), C 変化と関係 (relationships: proportion, 割合, speed), D データの活用**

Each grade lists 「知識及び技能」 and 「思考力，判断力，表現力等」 for each item, then 〔数学的活動〕 (mathematical activities:
problem solving from daily life and from maths, explaining with 具体物，図，数，式), 〔用語・記号〕 (the words and signs to be
introduced that year) and 内容の取扱い (scope notes). 第3 also stresses: practise the 筆算 until secure (「筆算による計算の技能を
確実に身に付けることを重視する」), estimate results, use そろばん and concrete materials in the lower grades, link 1年 to the
pre-school 「幼児期の終わりまでに育ってほしい姿」 (and to 生活科 at the start of 1年), and programming experience in 5年
(drawing regular polygons).

Below: **Official** = quoted or closely paraphrased from the 2017 text; **Usual practice** = what Japanese textbooks commonly
do (UNCERTAIN in detail, textbook order varies). Present the first as 「学習指導要領では」 and the second as
「多くの教科書では」.

### 年長 (5〜6歳)

- **Official (幼稚園教育要領 「環境」, whole 3–6 period):** 「日常生活の中で数量や図形などに関心をもつ」; numbers through
  play and need ("幼児自身の必要感に基づく体験"), not drills. End-of-pre-school picture (「幼児期の終わりまでに育ってほしい姿」 ⑻):
  children enjoy numbers, shapes, signs and letters in play and daily life and use them when they need them. There is **no
  maths subject, no textbook and no test**; MEXT promotes a bridge between 5歳児 and 1年生 (「幼保小の架け橋プログラム」).
- **Usual practice (UNCERTAIN, varies a lot by 園):** counting objects to 10 and reciting beyond (often to 20 or 100 in the bath:
  「おふろで100まで数える」 is a classic home habit); recognising digits 0–9; 「いくつといくつ」 with fingers and objects; sharing
  equally (おやつを分ける); comparing more/fewer, longer/shorter; shapes (まる・さんかく・しかく); 何時 on the clock (some children);
  calendar and 曜日 in 当番 routines; playing shop with toy money (おみせやさんごっこ).
- 就学時健康診断 (school health check, in autumn before 1年) and the purchase of the **算数セット** (おはじき, ブロック, 数え棒,
  とけい, 計算カード, 色板 etc., every item labelled with the child's name: 「名前つけ」 is a famous parent chore) happen in this year.

### 小学1年生 (6〜7歳)

**Official:**
- Numbers: comparing by matching (one-to-one); counting and writing quantities and order; ordering numbers and placing them on a
  **数直線**; seeing a number as the sum or difference of others; **two-digit numbers** (「２位数の表し方について理解すること」);
  "簡単な場合について，３位数の表し方を知ること"; **seeing numbers in tens** (「数を，十を単位としてみること」); grouping and sharing.
- Addition and subtraction: meaning, writing number sentences; **「１位数と１位数との加法及びその逆の減法の計算が確実にできること」**
  (all sums to 9 + 9 = 18 and the matching subtractions, including 繰り上がり and 繰り下がり); simple cases with two-digit numbers.
- Shape: recognising shapes of objects, making and taking apart shapes, position words (「前後，左右，上下」).
- Measurement: comparing length, area (広さ) and capacity (かさ) **directly and indirectly**, and with non-standard units ("その
  幾つ分"). No standard units yet.
- Time: **「日常生活の中で時刻を読むこと」**.
- Data: showing numbers of things in **simple pictures and diagrams** (絵グラフ).
- 〔用語・記号〕: 一の位 十の位 ＋ − ＝.

**Usual practice (UNCERTAIN):** numbers to 20 in 1学期, then to 100 and up to about 120 (the 3位数 "簡単な場合"); 「いくつといくつ」
(number bonds to 10) early; たし算 with 繰り上がり (9 + 4, using 「さくらんぼ計算」) and ひき算 with 繰り下がり (12 − 9, 減加法) in
2学期–3学期; 計算カード drilled at home as homework; clock: 「なんじ」「なんじはん」, then 「なんじなんぷん」 later in the year;
three-number sums (3つのかずのけいさん); simple 文章題 (word problems) with pictures; 「ふえると いくつ」「のこりは いくつ」「ちがいは
いくつ」. Money is used as a model (10円玉 for tens) in some books. **No 筆算 in 1年** (it starts in 2年).

### 小学2年生 (7〜8歳)

**Official:**
- Numbers: counting in equal groups; **numbers to four digits** (「４位数までについて，十進位取り記数法による数の表し方及び数の大小や順序」)
  and **1万** (内容の取扱い); seeing numbers as tens and hundreds; a number as the product of others; **simple fractions**
  (「½，⅓など簡単な分数について知ること」: the fractions are images in the MEXT PDF; the wording ½，⅓ is as quoted by
  prefectural board materials; the 解説 (p.106) describes activities making ½, ¼, ⅛ by folding. Write 「½や⅓、¼などの簡単な
  分数」 and introduce them by folding paper or tape).
- Addition and subtraction: two-digit numbers **with the 筆算** (「それらの筆算の仕方について理解すること」); simple three-digit cases;
  properties (交換法則, 結合法則); the relation between addition and subtraction; ( ) and □ may be used; estimate results.
- **Multiplication (乗法)**: meaning, number sentences, simple properties (how the product grows when the multiplier goes up by 1;
  the commutative law), **「乗法九九について知り，１位数と１位数との乗法の計算が確実にできること」**, simple two-digit × one-digit
  cases. **Division is not in 2年.**
- Shape: 三角形, 四角形; 正方形, 長方形, 直角三角形; boxes (面, 辺, 頂点); tiling with squares and rectangles.
- Measurement: **長さ mm, cm, m** and **かさ mL, dL, L** (dL, the decilitre, is a Japanese school favourite: 1 L = 10 dL,
  1 dL = 100 mL); estimating and choosing units.
- Time: **「日，時，分について知り，それらの関係を理解すること」** (1日 = 24時間, 1時間 = 60分; 午前・午後 and 正午 are taught here in
  textbooks).
- Data: **simple tables and graphs** (簡単な表やグラフ, usually ○ or ■ graphs).
- 〔用語・記号〕: 直線 直角 頂点 辺 面 単位 × ＞ ＜.

**Usual practice (UNCERTAIN):** 筆算 of たし算 and ひき算 in 1学期; **九九 in 2学期 (roughly October to December)**, introduced
段 by 段 (often the 5の段 and 2の段 first, then 3, 4, then 6, 7, 8, 9 and 1: order varies), recited aloud (「九九の暗唱」) and
checked one by one by the teacher, often with a 九九カード and a sticker or stamp card at home (「九九チャレンジ」 and 「合格」 checks
are class practice, not a national test); the 九九表 and its patterns, and extending it (e.g. 3 × 10, 12 × 4) in 3学期;
「何時間後」 problems; 1000 and 10000 with 100円玉 and 1000円札 pictures in some books.

### 小学3年生 (8〜9歳)

**Official:**
- Numbers: **万の単位** (to 千万) and **1億** (内容の取扱い); 10, 100, 1000 times and one tenth (1/10) of a number.
- Addition and subtraction: **3- and 4-digit 筆算**; reliable calculation; easy ones **mentally (暗算)** (内容の取扱い:
  「簡単な計算は暗算でできるよう配慮する」); estimation.
- Multiplication: **2- and 3-digit × 1- and 2-digit numbers, with the 筆算**; multiplying by 0; commutative, associative and
  distributive laws.
- **Division (除法)**: meaning (sharing and grouping: 等分除・包含除), **remainders (余り)**, link with multiplication and
  subtraction; **「除数と商が共に１位数である除法の計算が確実にできること」** (one 九九 fact: 23 ÷ 4 = 5 あまり 3); simple cases with a
  one-digit divisor and a two-digit quotient (e.g. 69 ÷ 3, 80 ÷ 4).
- **Decimals (小数)**: using decimals for parts of a unit; **the 1/10 position (1/10の位)**; adding and subtracting decimals to
  tenths. 0.1 and ⅒ linked on a number line.
- **Fractions (分数)**: fractions for equal parts; **unit fractions** (分数は単位分数の幾つ分); simple addition and subtraction
  (same denominator, e.g. ⅖ + ⅕ = ⅗).
- Expressions: using **□** for an unknown quantity and writing the relation as a number sentence (□ + 15 = 40).
- **そろばん**: showing numbers and simple addition and subtraction on the abacus.
- Shape: **二等辺三角形, 正三角形**, angles (角) as part of shapes; **円 (中心, 半径, 直径) and 球**; drawing with ruler and compass.
- Measurement: **km**, **g, kg**, and touching on **t** and the prefixes キロ (k) and ミリ (m); choosing scales and instruments.
- Time: **秒**; **finding times and durations needed in daily life** (「日常生活に必要な時刻や時間を求めること」).
- Data: classifying data into tables; **bar graphs (棒グラフ)**, including scales of 2, 5, 20, 50 and combined bar graphs.
- 〔用語・記号〕: 等号 不等号 小数点 1/10の位 数直線 分母 分子 ÷.

**Usual practice (UNCERTAIN):** わり算 starts early in 1学期; 「あまりのあるわり算」 next; 暗算 of 2-digit ± 2-digit; the 時刻と時間
unit (「30分後は何時何分」); 大きい数 with 一万, 十万; 重さ with a 台ばかり (kitchen scale).

### 小学4年生 (9〜10歳)

**Official:**
- Numbers: **億 and 兆**, deepening place value; mention that big numbers are sometimes **separated every three digits**
  (内容の取扱い ⑴: 「３桁ごとに区切りを用いる場合があることに触れる」).
- **暗算** (内容の取扱い ⑵, as in 3年): 「簡単な計算は暗算でできるよう配慮する」 and 「暗算を筆算や見積りに生かすよう配慮する」.
- **概数 (rounded numbers)**: when they are used, **四捨五入** (rounding half up), estimating results of all four operations.
- **Division**: **one- and two-digit divisors, two- and three-digit dividends, with the 筆算** (わり算の筆算);
  **（被除数）＝（除数）×（商）＋（余り）**; properties (multiplying or dividing both numbers by the same number keeps the quotient);
  4年 also includes integer ÷ integer giving a decimal quotient.
- **Decimals**: decimals to express "how many times"; decimals work like whole numbers (place value, 1/100 and 1/1000 positions in
  textbooks); **adding and subtracting decimals**; **decimal × whole number and decimal ÷ whole number**.
- **Fractions**: **大きさの等しい分数** (equivalent fractions, simple cases); **adding and subtracting fractions with the same
  denominator** (including 帯分数). 〔用語〕: **真分数, 仮分数, 帯分数**.
- Expressions: order of operations with mixed operations and ( ); **formulas** (公式, e.g. area of a rectangle = たて × 横);
  □ and △ for quantities.
- Properties of the four operations; **そろばん** (addition and subtraction).
- Shape: **平行・垂直**; **平行四辺形, ひし形, 台形**; **立方体, 直方体**, 見取図, 展開図; describing **position** (位置) with numbers
  (grid and 3D: "横3, たて2, 高さ1").
- **Area**: units **cm², m², km²** (and touching on **a, ha**); area of squares and rectangles by calculation.
- **Angles**: angle as amount of turn; unit **度 (°)**; measuring with a protractor (分度器).
- 変化と関係: two quantities that change together, shown in tables, number sentences and **line graphs (折れ線グラフ)**;
  **simple 割合** (comparing two relations using "how many times": new in 2017).
- Data: sorting data by **two criteria** (二次元の表); **line graphs**; avoiding omissions and double counting.
- 〔用語・記号〕: 和 差 積 商 以上 以下 未満 真分数 仮分数 帯分数 平行 垂直 対角線 平面.

**Usual practice (UNCERTAIN):** わり算の筆算 (one-digit divisor) in 1学期 and two-digit divisor in 2学期 (the hardest unit of
the primary years for many children: 「仮の商」 and correcting it); 「がい数」 with populations; 面積 with tiles and 1 m² of
newspaper.

### 小学5年生 (10〜11歳)

**Official:**
- Whole numbers: **偶数・奇数**; **約数・倍数** (〔用語〕 **最大公約数, 最小公倍数**, taught through concrete situations, not
  formal procedure). (**素数 is not in 小学校**: it is 中1.)
- 10, 100, 1000 times, 1/10, 1/100 of a number by moving the decimal point.
- **Decimal × decimal, decimal ÷ decimal** (meaning, calculation, the size of the remainder).
- Fractions: whole numbers and decimals as fractions and fractions as decimals; **a division as a fraction** (2 ÷ 3 = ⅔);
  **equivalent fractions by multiplying/dividing numerator and denominator** (〔用語〕 **約分, 通分**); comparing; **adding and
  subtracting fractions with different denominators**.
- Expressions: two quantities and simple relations (e.g. 正方形の辺の数と周りの長さ).
- Shape: **合同 (congruence)**; properties of polygons (angle sums of triangles and quadrilaterals in textbooks: 180°, 360°);
  **正多角形** with the circle (and **programming** to draw them, 第3 2⑵); **円周率** (meaning, use **3.14**); **角柱・円柱**
  (〔用語〕 底面, 側面).
- **Area by calculation**: triangle, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezium (三角形 = 底辺 × 高さ ÷ 2…).
- **Volume**: **cm³, m³**; volume of cubes and cuboids.
- 変化と関係: **比例** (simple cases); **単位量当たりの大きさ** (per-unit quantities: population density, price per item) and
  **速さ** (speed: 時速, 分速, 秒速 in textbooks); **割合** and **百分率 (%)**, touching on **歩合** (割, 分, 厘).
- Data: **円グラフ・帯グラフ** (pie and strip graphs); statistical problem-solving cycle; **平均 (mean)**.
- 〔用語・記号〕: 最大公約数 最小公倍数 通分 約分 底面 側面 比例 %.

**Usual practice (UNCERTAIN):** 割合 is famously hard (「くらべる量 ÷ もとにする量 ＝ 割合」, often with a 「く・も・わ」 diagram or a
数直線 / テープ図: which one the class uses varies); discounts (「2割引」「30%引き」) and 消費税 (10%) as contexts; 速さ with
「は・じ・き」 (some teachers discourage it: UNCERTAIN).

### 小学6年生 (11〜12歳)

**Official:**
- **Fraction × fraction and fraction ÷ fraction** (including by whole numbers); dividing as multiplying by the **reciprocal**
  (逆数); whole numbers and decimals brought into fraction calculation.
- **Letters for numbers**: **a, x** instead of □ and △ (文字を用いた式: x × 4 = 60).
- Shape: **縮図・拡大図** (scale drawings, enlargements); **対称な図形** (〔用語〕 **線対称, 点対称**, 対称の軸, 対称の中心);
  **approximate shapes and areas** of real objects; **area of a circle** (半径 × 半径 × 3.14); **volume of prisms and
  cylinders** (底面積 × 高さ).
- 変化と関係: **比例** (meaning, properties, using it to solve problems) and **反比例**; **比** (ratio, 〔記号〕 **：**, 比の値,
  equal ratios).
- Data: **代表値** (〔用語〕 **平均値, 中央値, 最頻値**), **ドットプロット**, frequency tables and graphs (度数分布表, 柱状グラフ;
  〔用語〕 階級); statistical problem solving and thinking critically about conclusions; **起こり得る場合** (listing possible
  outcomes systematically: arrangements and combinations, without omissions or overlaps).
- **Negative numbers, primes, π as a symbol and equations with x on both sides are not in 小学校** (they start in 中1).

**Usual practice (UNCERTAIN):** 全国学力・学習状況調査 in April (see section 3); review of all primary 算数 in 3学期; 「算数の
まとめ」; 速さ revisited; 比 with recipes (ドレッシング: 酢 と 油 の比).

## 4. Written methods (筆算) and how they are laid out

Japanese children call written calculations **「筆算」 (ひっ算)**, laid out vertically with digits aligned by place
(「位をそろえて書く」), worked from the 一の位. Textbooks stress the layout and saying each step aloud. Mental strategies in 1年
(さくらんぼ計算) come before 筆算 in 2年. There is **no ABN-style alternative and no grid method**: the 筆算 below is universal.
Common materials: ブロック (算数セット), 数え棒, おはじき, 位取り表 (place-value chart), 数直線, 100玉そろばん (some classes),
そろばん (3–4年), テープ図 (bar model), 線分図.

### 1年: 繰り上がりのあるたし算 / 繰り下がりのあるひき算 (mental, 「さくらんぼ計算」)

- **8 + 5**: 「8はあと2で10。5を2と3にわける。8と2で10。10と3で13」 (加数分解: split the second number). Children draw the split
  as two "cherries" under the 5 (hence 「さくらんぼ計算」, a nickname used widely in schools but not an official term). Splitting
  the first number (被加数分解: 3 + 9 → 2 と 1… ) is also shown in some books.
- **13 − 9**: **減加法** (the textbook default): 「13を10と3にわける。10から9をひいて1。1と3で4」. Also **減減法**: 13 − 3 = 10,
  10 − 6 = 4 (splitting 9 into 3 and 6). Which a class prefers: UNCERTAIN; both are correct.

### Addition: たし算の筆算 (繰り上がり)

```
     1 1          ← くり上がりの1 (written small, see note)
     3 4 7
   + 2 8 5
   -------
     6 3 2
```
Said aloud: 「一の位、7たす5は12。2をかいて、十の位に1くり上げる。十の位、1たす4たす8で13。3をかいて、百の位に1くり上げる。
百の位、1たす3たす2で6。」 Result 632. Note: **where the small carried 1 is written varies** (above the next column's top digit,
or small between the second number and the line); both are seen in textbooks (UNCERTAIN which is more common). In SVG/ASCII on
our pages, write it small above the next column and say "教科書によって書く場所が違います".

### Subtraction: ひき算の筆算 (繰り下がり) = decomposition

```
     4 2           ← small digits above: 5 crossed out → 4, 3 crossed out → 2
     5 3 2           (the ones digit is now 12, the tens digit 12)
   − 1 7 8
   -------
     3 5 4
```
「一の位、2から8はひけないので、十の位から1くり下げる。12ひく8は4。十の位は1くり下げたので2。2から7はひけないので、百の位から
1くり下げる。12ひく7は5。百の位は1くり下げたので4。4ひく1は3。」 Result 354. The crossed-out digit is replaced by a small
reduced digit above it (斜線で消して小さく書く). Check by addition (たしかめ算): 354 + 178 = 532. Zeros in the top number
(「0があるくり下がり」, e.g. 304 − 127) are a known stumbling block: 304 → 2 9 14.

### Multiplication: かけ算の筆算

```
       2 3 4
   ×     2 3
   ---------
       7 0 2      ← 234 × 3
     4 6 8        ← 234 × 2 (十の位), shifted one place left, no 0 written
   ---------
     5 3 8 2
```
Japanese layout **does not write the 0** at the end of the second partial product; it is shifted (「1けた左にずらして書く」).
Terms: かけられる数, かける数, 積. Carried digits (くり上がり) are written small or kept in the head (both seen). 九九 is read
aloud: 「にさんがろく」.

### Division: わり算の筆算 (long division, quotient on top)

Divisor on the left of a bracket 「厂」, dividend under it, **quotient written above the dividend**, each step's product and
subtraction written out underneath (Japanese schools always write the full steps; there is no short-division "bus stop" without
the working):
```
          2 1 4
       ┌───────
     4 ) 8 5 7
         8
         ─────
           5
           4
         ─────
           1 7
           1 6
         ─────
             1
```
The four steps, chanted: **「たてる → かける → ひく → おろす」** (estimate the quotient digit, multiply, subtract, bring down).
「8÷4で2をたてる。4×2=8をかく。8−8=0。5をおろす。5÷4で1をたてる…」 Answer written **857 ÷ 4 ＝ 214 あまり 1**. Check
(たしかめ算): **4 × 214 + 1 = 857** (the 4年 official relation 「（被除数）＝（除数）×（商）＋（余り）」). Terms: わられる数, わる数,
商, あまり; **「わりきれる」** (exact) / **「わりきれない」**. With a two-digit divisor children estimate a 「仮の商」 and correct it
(「商を1小さくする」). Decimal division moves the decimal point in both numbers (5年).

## 5. Notation

- **Decimal point**: 「.」 (小数点), 2.5 read 「にてんご」. Never a comma.
- **Thousands**: daily life uses a comma every three digits (1,280円, 12,000人). Japanese **reads** big numbers in **four-digit
  groups**: 万 (10⁴), 億 (10⁸), 兆 (10¹²): 12,345,678 = 「1234万5678」. The curriculum teaches the 万/億/兆 groups and only
  "touches on" three-digit commas in 4年 (内容の取扱い). Textbooks write 4-digit numbers without a separator (「1000」「4位数」)
  and often write big numbers with 万/億 or with small gaps. **Rule for our pages**: no separator up to 9999 (1000, 2026,
  9999); from 10000 either kanji units (「3万」, 「1億」) in curriculum statements or a comma (12,000) in prices and data; never in
  years, page numbers or times. Prices: 「1,280円」 with comma from 1,000円.
- Large numbers: 「1万」=10,000, 「10万」, 「100万」 (a million), 「1000万」, 「1億」 (100 million), 「1兆」 (a trillion, 10¹²). There is
  no single word for a billion: 10⁹ = 「10億」. Important on pages with big numbers.
- **Multiplication**: 「×」, read 「かける」. **Division**: 「÷」, read 「わる」 (÷ is in the 3年 〔記号〕). Both used throughout
  primary; 「・」 and fraction bars for division come in 中学校. Never "*" or ":" for division.
- **Equals**: 「＝」 (read 「は」 or 「イコール」: 「3たす4は7」). Comparison: 「＞」「＜」 (2年), 〔用語〕 不等号 (3年);
  「以上」「以下」「未満」 (4年, important for 概数). ≠ is not in the primary list: avoid it.
- **Ratio**: 「：」 (6年): 2：3, read 「2たい3」. 比の値 = ⅔.
- **Fractions**: stacked in books; **read denominator first**: ⅔ = 「3分の2」 (さんぶんのに), ½ = 「2分の1」. Mixed numbers
  (帯分数): 「1と3分の2」 written 1⅔. 真分数 (proper), 仮分数 (improper), 帯分数 (mixed). In running text use fraction
  characters (½, ⅔, ¹⁄₁₂) and, the first time, the reading in words (「3分の2（⅔）」). **Don't write 2/3 in running Japanese
  text**: it can read as a date (2月3日).
- **Percent**: 「25%」 (no space), read 「25パーセント」. 歩合: 1割 = 10%, 1分 = 1%, 1厘 = 0.1% (「3割引」 = 30% off).
- **Units**: metric only, with SI symbols and Japanese readings: mm, cm, m, km (ミリメートル…), g, kg, t, mL, dL, L (the
  curriculum writes litre as **L**, capital), cm², m², km², a, ha, cm³, m³. 「°」 for angles (「90°」, read 「90度」).
- **Numbers and spaces**: half-width digits; spaces around operators in sums on our site (「14 + 6 = 20」 as in i18n/ja.json),
  no spaces between Japanese words. Full-width 「、。：「」（）」 in text.
- **Times**: 「3時15分」 in words; digital 「15:30」 (half-width colon); 「午前8時」「午後3時」 (午前/午後 before the time).
- **Dates**: 「2026年10月10日」; the 和暦 「令和8年」 appears on school letters (one mention is enough). Weekdays 「月・火・水・木・金」.
- **Ordinals**: 「1番目」「3人目」「2つ目」.

## 7. Time

- Spoken and written: **3:00 「3時」 (「3時ちょうど」)**, **3:15 「3時15分」**, **3:30 「3時30分」 or 「3時半」**, **3:45 「3時45分」**
  (also 「4時15分前」, less common with children). Japanese uses **numbers of minutes, not "quarter past/to"**: 「3時10分」,
  「3時50分」 (「4時10分前」 also exists). 「〜分前」「〜分後」 for durations (3年). Readings: 「ふん/ぷん」 changes (1分 いっぷん,
  2分 にふん, 3分 さんぷん, 4分 よんぷん, 5分 ごふん, 6分 ろっぷん, 7分 ななふん, 8分 はっぷん (also はちふん), 9分 きゅうふん,
  10分 じゅっぷん / じっぷん): mention once on the time page. 4時 = 「よじ」, 7時 = 「しちじ」, 9時 = 「くじ」.
- **12-hour with 午前/午後** is the everyday spoken and school form: 「午前7時」「午後3時半」; 「正午」 (12:00 noon, 午後0時 / 午前12時
  confusion is real: say 正午 and 「夜の12時」). 「朝」「昼」「夕方」「夜」 are also common.
- **24-hour time** is used in writing: train and bus timetables, TV listings, opening hours (「10:00〜20:00」), school timetables in
  some schools, digital clocks. School: 2年 teaches 1日 = 24時間 and 午前/午後; reading 24-hour notation as such is mostly
  practised through 時刻表 and digital clocks (in which grade: UNCERTAIN, textbook-dependent). Pages: teach both, e.g.
  「15:30 は 午後3時30分」.
- Daily rhythm for examples: 登校 about 8:00–8:20, 朝の会, 1時間目 about 8:45, 中休み (about 20 min), 給食 about 12:20, 昼休み,

## 9. Form of address and tone

- **Polite です・ます** throughout, as in our site (`i18n/ja.json`), the store listings and the grown-up parts of the apps.
  No casual だ/である for the main text, no 敬語 overload (no 「〜させていただきます」 strings).
- Parents: **「保護者の方」** (section names, formal mentions), **「おうちの方」** (warm; the apps use 「おうちのかた」), child:
  **「お子さま」** (as on our site and in the apps), then 「子ども」 for children in general (e.g. 「子どもたちは…」). **Avoid
  「あなた」**: Japanese leaves the subject out (「〜してみてください」「〜するとよいでしょう」).
- Teachers: 「先生方」, 「先生向け」, 「クラス」, 「児童」 (the school word for primary pupils), 「子どもたち」.
- **Indie's voice** (`indie:` line, `<indie>`, challenge questions): casual and warm, as in the app (「〜だよ」「〜してね」「〜かな？」),
  but on these pages written with normal kanji (readers are adults; children may read along). E.g.
  「ハチの巣の部屋は正六角形。すき間なくならぶから、ミツバチにぴったりなんだよ。」 `<tip>` is in the plain polite adult voice.
- Warm, plain, direct, like a 担任の先生 talking to a parent at 個人面談. Avoid 「簡単です」「すぐできます」「だけ」 (≈ "just",
  "easy"); prefer 「少しずつ」「あせらずに」. Praise effort and process (「よく考えたね」), and 「まだ」 (not yet) language.
- No emoji. Full-width Japanese punctuation 「、。」, quotes 「」 (nested 『』), parentheses （）. Use the curriculum's terms
  first, then explain them (「学校では『くり上がり』と言います」).
- Kanji: write maths operation names as the textbooks do: **「たし算」「ひき算」「かけ算」「わり算」** (kana + 算). Grade-appropriate
  kanji don't matter for adult text, but avoid rare kanji; add readings in （） for terms parents may not read (「帯分数（たいぶんすう）」)
  once per page where useful.
