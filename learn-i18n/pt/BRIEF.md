## 1. The school system

| Stage | Years (local names) | Ages | Compulsory? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Educação Infantil: **creche** | berçário, maternal I, maternal II (names vary) | 0 to 3 | No (a right, not an obligation) |
| Educação Infantil: **pré-escola** | two years, named locally "Pré I / Pré II", "Infantil 4 / Infantil 5", "Pré-escola 1 / 2", in some private schools "Jardim I / Jardim II" or "Nível 4 / Nível 5" (names vary by network: UNCERTAIN which is most common) | 4 and 5 | **Yes** (LDB art. 4 I and art. 6: enrolment compulsory from 4) |
| Ensino Fundamental, **anos iniciais** | 1º, 2º, 3º, 4º, 5º ano | 6 to 10 (11 by the end of 5º) | Yes |
| Ensino Fundamental, **anos finais** | 6º, 7º, 8º, 9º ano | 11 to 14 | Yes |
| Ensino Médio | 1ª, 2ª, 3ª série | 15 to 17 | Yes |

- **Compulsory from age 4**: "educação básica obrigatória e gratuita dos 4 (quatro) aos 17 (dezessete) anos de
  idade, organizada da seguinte forma: a) pré-escola; b) ensino fundamental; c) ensino médio" (LDB art. 4 I). This is
  a big difference from England and Spain: **pré-escola is compulsory** in Brazil.
- Ensino Fundamental lasts **nine years, starting at 6** (LDB art. 32: "com duração de 9 (nove) anos… iniciando-se
  aos 6 (seis) anos de idade"). Older relatives may remember the eight-year "1ª a 8ª série" and "pré"/"alfabetização"
  before it; don't use "série" for Ensino Fundamental years (it survives only in Ensino Médio and in older speech).
- **Cut-off: 31 March** (Resolução CNE/CEB nº 2/2018). A child enters pré-escola in the year they turn 4 by 31 March,
  and 1º ano in the year they turn 6 by 31 March. So in 1º ano children are 6 at the start of the year (February) or
  turn 6 in March, and most turn 7 during the year: "6 a 7 anos". A Brazilian child in 1º ano is about the same age
  as an English Year 2 child.
- Writing years: **"1º ano", "2º ano"…** (masculine ordinal "º", no full stop; this is how the BNCC writes it);
  "primeiro ano" in speech. "Anos iniciais" (1º–5º) is what people call primary; older speech says "Fundamental I"
  (1º–5º) and "Fundamental II" (6º–9º), still very common among parents and schools (fine to use once, explained).
- In the anos iniciais, one class teacher teaches nearly everything: "**a professora**" / "o professor" (often
  called "professora polivalente" or "pedagoga"; children often say "prô" or "tia": don't use those on our pages).
  Specialist teachers start in 6º ano.
- Schools: "escola pública" (most anos iniciais are **"escolas municipais"**, run by the city; some "estaduais"),
  "escola particular" (private). Public schools get free textbooks ("livro didático", chosen through the federal PNLD).
- **Who sets the curriculum**: the BNCC is national and compulsory for all schools, public and private. Each state
  and municipality writes its own "currículo" or "referencial curricular" on top of it (e.g. "Currículo Paulista",
  "Currículo em Movimento" in the Distrito Federal), and each school its "projeto político-pedagógico" and plans.
  Pages should say "a BNCC prevê…" for national content and "depende da rede e da escola" where practice varies.

### Years our Learn section covers

The English section covers Reception to Year 6 (ages 4 to 11). By age, that is exactly Brazil's **pré-escola and
1º to 5º ano** (ages 4 to 11): all of pré-escola and all of the anos iniciais, which is where Brazil's "primary"
ends. 6º ano (11 to 12) is the start of the anos finais, with specialist teachers and the BNCC's EF06 content;
it is **not** covered (the 5º ano page can say briefly what comes next in 6º ano).

Pré-escola is one page, not two: the BNCC gives a single set of objectives for "crianças pequenas" (4 anos a 5 anos
e 11 meses) with no split between the two years, and local year names vary. The page can describe what usually
happens at 4 and at 5 (UNCERTAIN, usual practice) and the transition to 1º ano.

### School calendar and reports

- The school year runs with the **calendar year**: from early **February** (some networks late January) to
  **mid-December**, with **"férias de julho"** (two to three weeks in July) and the long summer holidays from
  mid-December to late January/February. LDB art. 24 I: at least **800 hours over at least 200 "dias letivos"**.
  Carnaval (February/March) is a break of a few days. Exact dates are set by each network ("calendário escolar"):
  say "fevereiro", "julho", "dezembro", never exact days.
- Terms: most networks split the year into **four "bimestres"**, some into three "trimestres" (UNCERTAIN which is
  more common; say "bimestre ou trimestre"). Reports: "**boletim**". Marks vary by network: a 0 to 10 scale with a
  pass mark ("média") of 5, 6 or 7, letter or word grades ("conceitos"), or a written report ("parecer descritivo"),
  which is usual in 1º and 2º ano (all UNCERTAIN, varies). Parents meet the teacher at "**reunião de pais**"
  ("reunião de pais e mestres"). Extra help: "**reforço**" (classes), "**recuperação**" (catch-up assessment).
- **Educação Infantil has no marks and no retention**: assessment is by "acompanhamento e registro do
  desenvolvimento das crianças, sem o objetivo de promoção, mesmo para o acesso ao ensino fundamental" (LDB art. 31 I).
- In 1º–2º ano the national priority is **"alfabetização"** (learning to read and write), and the BNCC says literacy
  should be the focus of the first two years. National policy: "Compromisso Nacional Criança Alfabetizada" (2023,
  reading). Many networks avoid holding children back ("reprovar", "repetir de ano") in the first years ("ciclo de
  alfabetização"; rules vary: UNCERTAIN).
- National dates children love: **Dia das Crianças, 12 October** (a national holiday, with Nossa Senhora Aparecida),
  Dia do Professor (15 October), **festa junina** (June/July, in almost every school).

## 2. What the curriculum expects, year by year

**How the official curriculum is built.** The BNCC sets the maths "habilidades" **year by year** (unlike Spain's
cycles), in **five "unidades temáticas": Números, Álgebra, Geometria, Grandezas e medidas, Probabilidade e
estatística**. Each habilidade has a code: EF (Ensino Fundamental) + year (01–05) + MA (Matemática) + number, e.g.
**EF03MA07**. The BNCC's aim is "**letramento matemático**" (mathematical literacy: reasoning, representing,
communicating, arguing). It also has eight "competências específicas de Matemática". Teachers know the codes;
parents don't, so pages can mention a code now and then for teachers, but explain in plain words.

Note on Álgebra: the BNCC puts "Álgebra" from **1º ano** (patterns, sequences, the idea of equality), which surprises
parents ("álgebra no 1º ano?"); explain that it means spotting patterns and understanding "=", not letters.

### Pré-escola (4 e 5 anos)

- **Official (BNCC, Educação Infantil)**: no subject called maths. The BNCC organises Educação Infantil in five
  "**campos de experiências**"; maths sits mainly in "**Espaços, tempos, quantidades, relações e transformações**".
  Objectives for "crianças pequenas" (4 anos a 5 anos e 11 meses), the maths ones:
  - EI03ET01 "Estabelecer relações de comparação entre objetos, observando suas propriedades."
  - EI03ET04 record observations and measures "usando múltiplas linguagens (desenho, registro por números ou escrita
    espontânea)".
  - EI03ET05 "Classificar objetos e figuras de acordo com suas semelhanças e diferenças."
  - EI03ET07 "Relacionar números às suas respectivas quantidades e identificar o antes, o depois e o entre em uma
    sequência."
  - EI03ET08 "Expressar medidas (peso, altura etc.), construindo gráficos básicos."
  - Also in "Corpo, gestos e movimentos" and "Espaços, tempos…": position and space words, time (yesterday/today/
    tomorrow, routines, calendar). Learning happens through "**interações e brincadeiras**" (the BNCC's two
    structuring axes).
- **Usual practice (UNCERTAIN, varies)**: counting objects to 10 at 4 and to 20 or beyond at 5; recognising and
  writing the "**numerais**" 0 to 9 (often "o traçado dos números"); one-to-one correspondence; more/less/same
  ("mais, menos, a mesma quantidade"); adding and taking away with objects; circle, square, triangle, rectangle;
  sorting by colour, shape, size; patterns ("sequências"); "**calendário**" and "chamadinha" (counting who's here)
  routines; board games with a die.
- No marks, no tests, no retention (LDB art. 31). The BNCC asks for a "síntese das aprendizagens" to pass to the
  1º ano teacher.

### 1º ano (6 a 7 anos)

BNCC habilidades (paraphrased, with key wording in quotes):
- **Números**: numbers for quantity, order and as codes (EF01MA01); counting exactly or approximately, grouping
  (EF01MA02); estimating and comparing two collections of about 20 (EF01MA03); **counting collections "até 100
  unidades"** and recording the result (EF01MA04); comparing numbers "de até duas ordens", with and without the
  "**reta numérica**" (EF01MA05); "**fatos básicos da adição**" (EF01MA06); composing and decomposing two-digit
  numbers with materials (EF01MA07); addition and subtraction problems with numbers up to two digits, meanings
  "juntar, acrescentar, separar e retirar", with pictures or materials (EF01MA08).
- **Álgebra**: sorting by attributes (EF01MA09); patterns and missing elements in sequences (EF01MA10).
- **Geometria**: position words (right, left, in front, behind) (EF01MA11–12); 3D shapes (cone, cilindro, esfera,
  bloco retangular) in everyday objects (EF01MA13); "círculo, quadrado, retângulo e triângulo" (EF01MA14).
- **Grandezas e medidas**: comparing lengths, capacities, masses with words ("mais alto, mais baixo, mais
  comprido…") (EF01MA15); sequence of events in a day (EF01MA16); parts of the day, days of the week, months, using
  a calendar (EF01MA17); writing a date and finding the day of the week (EF01MA18); **"Reconhecer e relacionar
  valores de moedas e cédulas do sistema monetário brasileiro"** (EF01MA19).
- **Probabilidade e estatística**: chance words "acontecerá com certeza", "talvez aconteça", "é impossível acontecer"
  (EF01MA20); reading tables and simple column graphs (EF01MA21); a class survey with up to 30 items (EF01MA22).
- Usual practice (UNCERTAIN): sums to 10 then to 20 ("pares que formam 10"); counting in 2s, 5s, 10s; "dezena" and
  "unidade" with "material dourado" and "palitos" bundled in tens; "conta armada" without carrying late in the year;
  "antecessor e sucessor"; "par e ímpar"; o'clock ("horas exatas").

### 2º ano (7 a 8 anos)

- **Números**: comparing and ordering numbers "até a ordem de centenas", place value and zero (EF02MA01);
  estimating and counting collections up to 1000 (EF02MA02); comparing sets ("tem mais, tem menos, tem a mesma
  quantidade") (EF02MA03); composing and decomposing numbers "de até três ordens" (EF02MA04); **fatos básicos da
  adição e da subtração** for mental and written calculation (EF02MA05); addition and subtraction problems up to
  three orders (EF02MA06); **multiplication "por 2, 3, 4 e 5"** as "adição de parcelas iguais" (EF02MA07);
  "**dobro, metade, triplo e terça parte**" (EF02MA08).
- **Álgebra**: counting on and back from any number by a rule (EF02MA09); describing patterns (EF02MA10); missing
  elements (EF02MA11).
- **Geometria**: locating and moving with reference points, direction and sense (EF02MA12); sketching routes and
  plans (EF02MA13); "cubo, bloco retangular, pirâmide, cone, cilindro e esfera" (EF02MA14); plane figures (EF02MA15).
- **Grandezas e medidas**: lengths with non-standard and standard units "**metro, centímetro e milímetro**"
  (EF02MA16); capacity and mass "**litro, mililitro, grama e quilograma**" (EF02MA17); intervals between dates on a
  calendar (EF02MA18); measuring a duration with a "**relógio digital**", start and end times (EF02MA19); equivalence
  between coins and notes (EF02MA20).
- **Probabilidade e estatística**: "muito prováveis, pouco prováveis, improváveis e impossíveis" (EF02MA21);
  "tabelas de dupla entrada", "gráficos de colunas simples ou barras" (EF02MA22–23).
- Usual practice (UNCERTAIN): "conta armada" with "**vai um**" (carrying) and with "**empresta**" (borrowing/
  regrouping), often starting this year or in 3º; the tabuada do 2, 3, 4, 5 (and 10) begun; "meia hora" on analogue
  clocks.

### 3º ano (8 a 9 anos)

- **Números**: read, write and compare "**até a ordem de unidade de milhar**" (to 9 999) (EF03MA01); composing and
  decomposing four-digit numbers (EF03MA02); "**fatos básicos da adição e da multiplicação**" for mental or written
  calculation (EF03MA03); numbers on the number line, + and − as moves right/left (EF03MA04); mental and written
  procedures for + and − (EF03MA05); + and − problems with "juntar, acrescentar, separar, retirar, comparar e
  completar" (EF03MA06); **multiplication "por 2, 3, 4, 5 e 10"**, as equal groups and "disposição retangular"
  (arrays) (EF03MA07); **division of a number by another "até 10", with remainder zero and not zero**, as fair
  sharing and as measuring (EF03MA08); "**metade, terça, quarta, quinta e décima partes**" as quotients (EF03MA09).
- **Álgebra**: sequences made by adding or subtracting the same number, finding the rule (EF03MA10); the idea of
  equality, different sums with the same result (EF03MA11).
- **Geometria**: routes, "croquis", "maquetes" (EF03MA12); 3D shapes and nets ("planificações") (EF03MA13–14);
  classifying "triângulo, quadrado, retângulo, trapézio e paralelogramo" by sides and vertices (EF03MA15); congruent
  figures on grids (EF03MA16).
- **Grandezas e medidas**: a measure depends on the unit (EF03MA17); choosing units and instruments (EF03MA18);
  m, cm, mm (EF03MA19); L, mL, kg, g, **mg**, on labels and packaging (EF03MA20); comparing areas by superposition
  (EF03MA21); **analogue and digital clocks**, start, end and duration (EF03MA22); hours and minutes, minutes and
  seconds (EF03MA23); comparing and exchanging money in buying and selling (EF03MA24).
- **Probabilidade e estatística**: all possible outcomes, more or less likely (EF03MA25); double-entry tables, bar
  and column graphs, "maior e menor frequência" (EF03MA26–27); survey up to 50 items (EF03MA28).
- Usual practice (UNCERTAIN): the column methods with "vai um" and "empresta" secured; multiplication by one digit
  with the algorithm; division "na chave" by one digit; tabuada to 10 ("tabuada do 6 ao 9" often in 3º–4º).

### 4º ano (9 a 10 anos)

- **Números**: read, write and order "**até a ordem de dezenas de milhar**" (to 99 999) (EF04MA01); any number as
  sums of multiples of powers of ten (EF04MA02); + and − with algorithms and estimation (EF04MA03); relations
  between + and −, × and ÷ (EF04MA04); properties of operations (EF04MA05); multiplication as equal groups, arrays and
  proportionality (EF04MA06); **division with a divisor of "no máximo dois algarismos"** (EF04MA07); simple counting
  problems (all the pairs) (EF04MA08); **unit fractions "1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5, 1/10 e 1/100"** on the number line
  (EF04MA09); **decimals: "décimos e centésimos" linked to the "sistema monetário brasileiro"** (EF04MA10).
- **Álgebra**: sequences of multiples (EF04MA11); numbers that leave the same remainder (EF04MA12); inverse
  operations, with a calculator if needed (EF04MA13); an equality stays true when the same number is added or
  subtracted on both sides (EF04MA14); **the unknown number in an equality** with the four operations (EF04MA15).
- **Geometria**: maps, "planta baixa", grids, "paralelas, perpendiculares" (EF04MA16); prisms and pyramids and
  their nets (EF04MA17); **right angles** ("ângulos retos e não retos") with folding or a set square (EF04MA18);
  **reflection symmetry** (EF04MA19).
- **Grandezas e medidas**: lengths including **perimeter**, masses, capacities (EF04MA20); **area on squared paper**
  by counting squares and half squares (EF04MA21); hours, minutes and seconds, durations (EF04MA22); **temperature in
  "grau Celsius"**, comparing Brazilian regions (EF04MA23); a column graph of daily max/min temperatures, also with a
  spreadsheet (EF04MA24); buying, selling and "formas de pagamento", "**troco**" and "**desconto**", "consumo ético,
  consciente e responsável" (EF04MA25).
- **Probabilidade e estatística**: more likely events, without fractions (EF04MA26); tables, column and pictographic
  graphs, writing a short summary (EF04MA27); surveys with numerical variables, grouped column graphs (EF04MA28).
- Usual practice (UNCERTAIN): the whole tabuada (1 to 10) expected by heart by the end of 4º; multiplication by two
  digits; "divisão por dois algarismos" in the chave.

### 5º ano (10 a 11 anos)

- **Números**: read, write and order "**até a ordem das centenas de milhar**" (to 999 999) (EF05MA01); decimals:
  reading, writing, ordering, number line (EF05MA02); fractions smaller and bigger than 1, as division and as part of
  a whole (EF05MA03); **"Identificar frações equivalentes"** (EF05MA04); comparing and ordering fractions and decimals
  (EF05MA05); **percentages "10%, 25%, 50%, 75% e 100%"** linked to "décima parte, quarta parte, metade, três quartos
  e um inteiro", for example in "educação financeira" (EF05MA06); + and − with naturals and decimals (EF05MA07);
  × and ÷ with naturals and decimals, **multiplier and divisor natural numbers** (EF05MA08); the multiplicative
  principle with tree diagrams and tables (EF05MA09).
- **Álgebra**: equality stays true with all four operations on both sides (EF05MA10); problems as an equality with
  one unknown (EF05MA11); **direct proportion** (price and quantity, recipes, map scales) (EF05MA12); sharing in
  unequal parts, e.g. one part double the other (EF05MA13).
- **Geometria**: maps and grids as early coordinates (EF05MA14); the "**plano cartesiano (1º quadrante)**"
  (EF05MA15); solids and nets (EF05MA16); polygons by sides, vertices and angles, drawing them (EF05MA17); enlarging
  and reducing on grids (EF05MA18).
- **Grandezas e medidas**: problems with length, area, mass, time, temperature and capacity, **converting between
  common units** (EF05MA19); same perimeter, different area and the reverse (EF05MA20); **volume by stacking cubes**
  (EF05MA21).
- **Probabilidade e estatística**: all outcomes, equally likely or not (EF05MA22); **probability** of an outcome when
  outcomes are equally likely (EF05MA23); tables, column and **line graphs** ("gráficos de linhas") (EF05MA24–25).
- What the BNCC does **not** put in anos iniciais (it comes in 6º ano): adding and subtracting fractions with
  different denominators, multiplying fractions, negative numbers, prime numbers and "MMC/MDC", powers, the "regra de
  três" as a method. Many textbooks introduce "números primos", "múltiplos e divisores" or adding fractions with the
  same denominator in 5º ano (UNCERTAIN): present as "alguns livros já trazem…", not as required. Decimals to
  "milésimos" in 5º are common in textbooks (UNCERTAIN; the BNCC text says only "números racionais na forma decimal").

## 4. Written methods ("algoritmos", "conta armada") and how they are laid out

Children say "**fazer a conta**", "**armar a conta**" ("conta armada" = vertical written method); teachers say
"algoritmo convencional" or "algoritmo usual". Contas armadas are usually done on "**caderno quadriculado**" (squared
exercise book), one digit per square, units under units ("unidade embaixo de unidade"). Before the algorithm, the BNCC
asks for "estratégias pessoais" and mental calculation; textbooks use **material dourado** (base-10 blocks,
Montessori) and the "**quadro de ordens**" / "quadro valor de lugar (QVL)" (place-value chart headed C D U).
Decomposition ("decomposição": 347 = 300 + 40 + 7) is often written as an intermediate method.

### Addition: "com reserva" ("vai um")

```
   1 1          ← the carried 1s ("reserva"), written small above
   3 4 7
 + 2 8 5
 -------
   6 3 2
```
Said aloud: "7 mais 5 é 12: fica 2 e **vai um**. 4 mais 8 é 12, mais 1 é 13: fica 3 e vai um. 3 mais 2 é 5, mais 1
é 6." Result 632. Textbook terms: "adição com reagrupamento" or "com troca" (10 unidades trocadas por 1 dezena).
Terms: "parcelas", "soma" or "total".

### Subtraction: "com recurso" ("empresta um", decomposition/regrouping)

```
   4 12 12     ← written small above after regrouping: 5 C → 4 C; 3 D → 2 D → 12 D; 2 U → 12 U
   5  3  2     (the 5 and the 3 are crossed out in the exercise book)
 − 1  7  8
 ---------
   3  5  4
```
"2 menos 8 não dá: **pego emprestado** 1 dezena do 3, que vira 2; 12 menos 8 é 4. 2 menos 7 não dá: pego 1 centena
do 5, que vira 4; 12 menos 7 é 5. 4 menos 1 é 3." Result 354 (check: 354 + 178 = 532, the "**prova real**").
Textbook terms: "subtração com reagrupamento", "desagrupar", "trocar 1 dezena por 10 unidades". Terms: "minuendo",
"subtraendo", "**resto**" or "**diferença**". The decomposition method is the standard in current Brazilian books.
Some older adults learnt a compensation method (adding 1 to the bottom digit); UNCERTAIN how common, don't show it.

### Multiplication

```
       2 3 4
     ×   2 3
     -------
       7 0 2      ← 234 × 3
 +   4 6 8        ← 234 × 2 dezenas, moved one place left (gap or a 0 in the units)
     -------
     5 3 8 2
```
Carried digits are written small above, as in addition ("vai"). Terms: "**fatores**", "**produto**". Times tables are
said "três vezes quatro, doze" and written 3 × 4 = 12.

### Division: "método da chave" ("conta de dividir na chave")

Dividend on the left, divisor on the right inside the "**chave**" (a corner: a vertical line and a line under the
divisor); quotient under the divisor; subtractions under the dividend.

Long form ("processo longo", subtractions written), usually first:
```
  8 5 7 | 4
− 8     |-------
  ---     2 1 4
  0 5
  − 4
  ---
    1 7
  − 1 6
  -----
      1
```
Short form ("processo breve" / "curto", only remainders written), later:
```
  8 5 7 | 4
  0 5     2 1 4
    1 7
      1
```
"8 dividido por 4 dá 2; 2 vezes 4, 8; para 8, zero. Baixo o 5: 5 dividido por 4 dá 1; sobra 1. Baixo o 7: 17
dividido por 4 dá 4; 4 vezes 4, 16; sobra 1." Quotient 214, remainder 1. Check ("prova real"): **dividendo = divisor
× quociente + resto**: 4 × 214 + 1 = 857. Terms: "**dividendo, divisor, quociente, resto**"; "divisão exata"
(resto zero) and "divisão não exata". Some textbooks also teach a "processo por estimativas" (subtracting easy
multiples of the divisor, like chunking) before the short form (UNCERTAIN how common; mention as one route at most).
England's "bus stop" layout and grid method are not used: don't show them.

## 5. Notation

- **Decimal comma**: 2,5 (read "dois vírgula cinco" or, in class, "dois inteiros e cinco décimos"). Never a point.
- **Thousands**: in everyday Brazil and in prices, a **point**: 1.000, 25.000, R$ 1.250,00. Many textbooks follow the
  SI and write a **space**: 1 000, 25 300 (UNCERTAIN which is more common in current books). For our pages: **point
  for 5+ digit numbers and in money; 4-digit numbers without a separator** ("até 9999") or with a point ("9.999"):
  pick the point consistently (1.000) except in years (2026) and times. Never a comma for thousands.
- **Multiplication**: "**×**" in the anos iniciais (read "vezes"); "·" appears in anos finais. Never "*".
- **Division**: in a line, **"÷"** is the usual sign in anos iniciais books (read "dividido por"); ":" also appears
  (UNCERTAIN which is more frequent; recommended for our pages: ÷). Written division uses the "chave".
- **Minus** "−" read "menos"; **equals** "=" read "igual a" or "é" (3 + 4 é igual a 7). Comparison: "<" "menor que",
  ">" "maior que", "≠" "diferente de".
- **Fractions**: ½, ¾ (fraction characters fine). Read: "um meio" (also "metade"), "um terço", "um quarto", "um
  quinto", "um sexto", "um sétimo", "um oitavo", "um nono", "um décimo", then **"avos"**: "um onze avos", "cinco doze
  avos"; "um centésimo", "um milésimo". The BNCC writes 1/2, 1/3. Terms: "numerador", "denominador", "fração
  própria/imprópria", "número misto" (taught later: UNCERTAIN in anos iniciais), "frações equivalentes".
- **Decimals**: "**décimos, centésimos, milésimos**" (masculine in Brazil); "parte inteira" and "parte decimal".
- **Percent**: "25%" with **no space** (common Brazilian practice), read "vinte e cinco por cento".
- **Ordinals**: 1º, 2º, 1ª, 2ª (no full stop).
- **Large numbers**: "**bilhão**" in Brazil = 1.000.000.000 (10⁹), like English (unlike Portugal's "mil milhões").
  "Milhão" = 10⁶, "trilhão" = 10¹².
- **Units**: metric only. m, cm, mm, km; kg, g, mg; **L and mL** (capital L is usual in Brazilian books and labels;
  "l" also correct); °C. Spell "quilômetro", "milímetro", "quilograma" (often "quilo"). Time: "h", "min", "s".
- **Money**: "**R$ 3,45**" (symbol before the amount, with a space); read "três reais e quarenta e cinco centavos",
  informally "três e quarenta e cinco". "R$ 0,50" = "cinquenta centavos". Prices often end in ",99" or ",90".
- **Times**: written "**15h30**" (very common in Brazil, also "15h" and "15h30min"), digital clocks show 15:30. Use
  15h30 in running text and 15:30 when showing a digital display.
- **Dates**: day/month/year, 10/10/2026, "10 de outubro de 2026". Brazilian calendars usually **start the week on
  Sunday** ("domingo"); weekdays are "segunda-feira… sexta-feira", "sábado", "domingo".
