# India briefs: fact check (in + hi)

Checked on **2026-10-14** against official or primary sources only, for `learn-i18n/in/BRIEF.md` (Indian English) and
`learn-i18n/hi/BRIEF.md` (Hindi). Each row records a page that was actually opened. "Brief" gives the claim as the
brief stated it before this check. Verdicts:

- **confirmed**: an official page says it;
- **corrected**: an official page says something different (the correction is given);
- **still uncertain**: no official page could be opened, or the sources didn't settle it.

**How the NCERT books were checked.** The current NCERT PDFs (all marked "Reprint 2026-27") were downloaded from
`https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/<code>.pdf` and searched as text: English `aejm` (Class 1), `bejm` (Class 2), `cemm`
(Class 3), `demm` (Class 4), `eemm` (Class 5); Hindi `ahjm`, `bhjm`, `chmm`, `dhmm`, `ehmm`. `…1ps.pdf` is the front
matter; `…101.pdf`, `…102.pdf`… are the chapters. The Hindi PDFs use a legacy font, so their extracted text is
partly garbled: every Hindi term below was either clear in the text or checked by rendering the page as an image and
reading it. "Not found" means a full-text search of all chapters of that book found no match. Words inside pictures
can be missed this way, so "not found" is weaker evidence than a match.

Abbreviations: MoE = Ministry of Education; PIB = Press Information Bureau.

**Tally (rows 1–56):** 34 confirmed (4 of them only in part: rows 20, 26, 41, 42), 12 corrected, 10 still uncertain.
Hindi terms (last section): 8 corrections to the hi brief (stage names, FLN wording, NCF-FS Hindi name, activity
headings, पुनः समूहीकरण, dotted unit abbreviations, four number names, हजार without nukta), about a dozen
confirmations, 5 still uncertain.

**Most important for page writing:** the new NCERT books for Classes 1–5 contain no decimals, percentages, lakhs or
crores, Roman numerals, HCF/LCM or degrees, and stop at five-digit numbers in Class 5 (rows 5, 13–17); NCERT teaches
division by partial quotients in the bracket layout (row 23); NCERT says "regroup" / "पुनः समूहीकरण", not carry/borrow
or हासिल/उधार (row 21, Hindi terms); NIPUN's Class 1 target is adding and subtracting within 9 and Class 2's is the
tables of 2, 3 and 4 (row 30); Uttar Pradesh kept no-detention as of Dec 2024 (row 37).
