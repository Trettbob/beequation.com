## 1. The school system

| Stage | Years (local names) | Ages (만 나이) | Compulsory? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 어린이집 (childcare, 0~5세) / 유치원 (kindergarten, 만 3~5세) | 유치원: "만 3세반", "만 4세반", "만 5세반" (classes usually have names, e.g. "해님반"); 어린이집 also runs the same 3~5세 classes | 3 to 6 | No. The three years before school are free by law ("초등학교 취학직전 3년의 유아교육은 무상", 유아교육법 제24조). Both 유치원 and 어린이집 follow the same **누리과정** for 만 3~5세. |
| 초등학교 (primary school) | 1학년 to 6학년 ("초등학교 1학년", spoken "초등 1학년", "초1"); grouped in the curriculum as 1~2학년군, 3~4학년군, 5~6학년군 | 6 to 12 | **Yes** (6 years, 초·중등교육법 제39조) |
| 중학교 (middle school) | 1학년 to 3학년 ("중1"…) | 12 to 15 | Yes |
| 고등학교 (high school) | 1학년 to 3학년 | 15 to 18 | No (free) |

- **School starts on 1 March** (학년도 = 1 March to the end of February, 초·중등교육법 제24조). Two 학기: 1학기 from
  1 March to a date the school sets (in July), 2학기 from then to the end of February (시행령 제44조). At least
  **190 school days** a year (시행령 제45조).
- **Compulsory entry:** "6세가 된 날이 속하는 해의 다음 해 3월 1일" (초·중등교육법 제13조①). So a class is everyone
  born in one **calendar year** (January to December): children born in 2019 started 1학년 in March 2026. Parents may
  start a year early or late (제13조②), but almost nobody does.
- Ages: in March of 1학년 children are 6 (born March to December) or 7 (born January or February), and most turn 7
  during the year. So: **1학년 = 만 6~7세**, 2학년 = 만 7~8세 … 6학년 = 만 11~12세. The 유치원 "만 5세반" is the
  year before school (children who turn 6 during that year): **만 5~6세**.
- **Age: always use 만 나이.** Since 28 June 2023 the international age is the legal standard (the apps already write
  "5~12세"). Many parents still say the old Korean age in speech ("여덟 살" for a 1학년 child, "7세반" for the
  만 5세반): never use it on our pages; write "만 6~7세" and, where a parent might be confused, simply give the 학년.
- Korean children are a little over a year older than English children with the same year number: 초등 1학년 ≈
  English Year 2 by age; 만 5세반 ≈ Year 1.
- Teachers: "담임 선생님" (class teacher; one teacher teaches almost every subject), "교과 전담 선생님" (specialist,
  e.g. 영어, 체육, sometimes 과학), "교장/교감 선생님". "학부모" (school word for parents), "보호자" (legal/app word).
  "학원" (private after-school academy, very common), "방과후 학교" (school-run after-school classes), "돌봄교실" /
  "늘봄학교" (after-school care in school; 늘봄학교 started with 1학년 in 2024, details UNCERTAIN: mention only in passing).
- The national curriculum is set by the state (now through the 국가교육위원회; the 2022 version was issued as 교육부
  고시). It is the **same for the whole country**; 시·도 교육청 add local guidelines. 1~2학년 maths textbooks are
  **국정** (one national book); 3~6학년 books are **검정** (several approved publishers, 학교 chooses). Every semester
  has two books: **《수학》** and the workbook **《수학 익힘》**. Lessons are 40 minutes in primary.
- Curriculum hours (UNCERTAIN figure for 1~2: from the 총론): 수학 256시간 in 1~2학년군, **272시간** in 3~4학년군
  (confirmed: "3~4학년군 수학과 수업 시수는 272시간", 비상 3-1 지도 계획) and 272 in 5~6학년군. Roughly 4 maths
  lessons a week.

### School calendar and reports

- 1학기: early March (입학식 / 개학 on 2 March, or the first weekday) to mid/late July. **여름방학** about four weeks
  (late July to mid/late August). 2학기: mid/late August to late December or early January. **겨울방학** in January
  and February (many schools now end the school year in early January and stay off until March, the "학년말 방학";
  practice varies, UNCERTAIN). Exact dates are set by each school: say "3월", "여름방학", "겨울방학", not dates.
- Holidays children know: 설날 (Lunar New Year, January/February, 세뱃돈), 어린이날 (5월 5일), 추석 (September/October,
  송편), 한글날 (10월 9일), 크리스마스. School events: 입학식, 학부모 상담 주간 (usually March/April and September,
  UNCERTAIN), 공개수업, 운동회/체육대회, 현장체험학습, 6학년 수학여행, 졸업식 (February or January).
- Reports: the school life record (학교생활기록부, 초·중등교육법 제25조) and a **통지표** (report) at the end of each
  semester. In primary there are **no marks out of 100, no ranks and no grades like A–E** on reports: subjects are
  described in sentences, and many schools add achievement levels in 3 or 4 steps such as "매우잘함 / 잘함 / 보통 /
  노력 요함" (format varies by school and 시·도, UNCERTAIN). Write "학교마다 통지표 양식은 달라요".
- There is no repeating a year in practice and no entrance test for 중학교: children are assigned to a local middle
  school (일부 특수 목적 중학교 aside).

## 2. What the curriculum expects, year by year

**How the official curriculum is built.** The 2022 maths curriculum gives content **per 학년군 (two-year band), not per
year**, in four 영역 that run from 1학년 to 중3: **수와 연산, 변화와 관계** (patterns, relations, ratio: early algebra),
**도형과 측정, 자료와 가능성** (data and probability). Five 교과 역량: 문제해결, 추론, 의사소통, 연결, 정보처리. Each
standard has "성취기준 해설" and "성취기준 적용 시 고려 사항" (what to include or avoid). Calculators may be used when
the aim is not calculation skill ("계산 기능을 숙달하는 것이 목적이 아닌 경우에는 계산기를 사용하게 할 수 있다").
Kindergarten has no maths subject: maths sits in the 누리과정 area 자연탐구.

The **per-year split comes from the textbooks** (semester books 1-1, 1-2 … 6-2), not from the curriculum text. 3~4학년
units below are from one publisher (비상교육, 2025); other publishers order units a little differently. 1~2학년 (국정)
and 5~6학년 units are from learning sites and publisher pages and are **UNCERTAIN in detail**. When writing a year page,
present the 학년군 standards as "교육과정에서 정한 내용" and the semester units as "교과서에서 보통 배우는 순서".

### 유치원 만 5세반 (만 5~6세)

- **Official (2019 개정 누리과정, 자연탐구 영역, 내용범주 "생활 속에서 탐구하기", common to 만 3~5세; the 2019 version
  deliberately has no age-by-age content):** "물체의 특성과 변화를 여러 가지 방법으로 탐색한다", "**물체를 세어 수량을
  알아본다**", "물체의 위치와 방향, 모양을 알고 구별한다", "일상에서 길이, 무게 등의 속성을 비교한다", "**주변에서
  반복되는 규칙을 찾는다**", "일상에서 모은 자료를 기준에 따라 분류한다", "도구와 기계에 대해 관심을 가진다". (The two
  bold lines were confirmed in a 2024 교육부 document; the others are from the 고시 as recalled: quote them only loosely,
  UNCERTAIN wording.) The 누리과정 is **놀이 중심** (play-based): no worksheets or written sums are required.
- **Usual practice (UNCERTAIN, varies by 기관):** counting objects to 10 and reciting further (often to 20 or more),
  both "하나, 둘, 셋" and "일, 이, 삼"; recognising digits 0 to 9; comparing (많다/적다, 길다/짧다, 무겁다/가볍다);
  adding and taking away with objects to about 10; 세모, 네모, 동그라미; sorting; patterns; days of the week and the
  calendar; many families also do "학습지" at home (don't name brands).
- No test; the 유치원 writes a 생활기록부 (유아교육법 제14조), descriptive.
- 2022 총론 also gives 1학년 an "입학 초기 적응 활동" period in March (UNCERTAIN detail): a good reassurance point on
  this page ("학교에서 천천히 시작해요").

### 1~2학년군: 1학년 and 2학년

**National (2022, [2수…]):**
- Numbers: "0과 100까지의 수 개념을 이해하고, 수를 세고 읽고 쓸 수 있다"; place value "일, 십, 백, 천의 자릿값";
  numbers **up to four digits** (네 자리 이하의 수), comparing; "하나의 수를 두 수로 분해하고 두 수를 하나의 수로 합성
  하는 활동" (가르기와 모으기) **within 20**. 짝수와 홀수 within 20. Counting by grouping and skip counting (묶어 세기,
  뛰어 세기). Tens shown as "10개씩 묶음과 낱개". Children should **not** be made to write numbers in Hangul words
  ("저학년 학생들의 한글 학습 정도를 고려하여 수를 '여덟', '마흔아홉', '칠십육', '첫째' 등과 같이 한글로 쓰게 하는 것은
  지양한다"; the same goes for comparison words like '짧다', '많다': children say them, the page should not ask them to
  write them) in 1~2학년.
- Addition and subtraction **in the two-digit range** ("합이 세 자리 수인 경우도 포함"), the relation between them, three
  numbers (using "합이 10이 되는 두 수를 먼저 더하고": making 10), "□가 사용된 덧셈식과 뺄셈식" at an intuitive level.
  Words: 더한다, 합한다, 뺀다, 덜어 낸다, 합, 차; "~보다 ~만큼 더 큰 수".
- **Multiplication:** meaning through "몇의 몇 배" and repeated addition (동수누가), 1의 곱 and 0의 곱, and
  "**곱셈구구를 이해하고, 한 자리 수의 곱셈을 할 수 있다**" ([2수01-11]): the tables **2단 to 9단 (up to 9 × 9)**,
  plus ×1 and ×0. Commutativity seen in the 곱셈표. **No division in 1~2학년** (it starts in 3학년).
- Symbols in 1~2학년: +, −, ×, =, >, < and the words 덧셈, 뺄셈, 곱셈, 짝수, 홀수.
- Patterns (변화와 관계): finding and making repeating and growing patterns in objects, designs and numbers; in 수
  배열표, 덧셈표, 곱셈표.
- Shape: 직육면체, 원기둥, 구 "모양" (box, cylinder, ball shapes) found in the classroom; 쌓기나무 with position words
  ("~의 앞", "~의 오른쪽", "~의 위", "2층"); 삼각형, 사각형, 원; 꼭짓점, 변; 칠교판 (tangram).
- Measurement: comparing length, capacity, weight and area (길다/짧다, 많다/적다, 무겁다/가볍다, 넓다/좁다); **cm and m**
  ("몇 m 몇 cm"), estimating ("약"), adding and subtracting lengths. Only cm and m: no g, kg, L until 3~4학년.
- **Time:** "시계를 보고 시각을 '몇 시 몇 분'까지 읽을 수 있다"; 1시간 = 60분; 1분, 1시간, 1일, 1주일, 1개월, 1년 using a
  calendar. Clocks: "몇 시", "몇 시 30분", "몇 시 몇 분", "몇 시 몇 분 전" (only simple cases like 5분 전, 10분 전).
- Data: sorting by a criterion, **표** (table), and simple graphs with ○, ×, / ("그래프").
- **Not in 1~2학년:** fractions, money, division, g/kg/L.

**1학년 (만 6~7세)**, 국정 textbook units (learning-site lists, UNCERTAIN):
- 1-1: 9까지의 수 · 여러 가지 모양 (상자·둥근기둥·공 모양) · 덧셈과 뺄셈 (가르기와 모으기, one-digit) · 비교하기 ·
  50까지의 수.
- 1-2: 100까지의 수 (짝수와 홀수) · 덧셈과 뺄셈(1) (two-digit, no 받아올림) · 여러 가지 모양 (△, □, ○ 모양) ·

**2학년 (만 7~8세)** (UNCERTAIN, as above):
- 2-1: 세 자리 수 · 여러 가지 도형 (삼각형, 사각형, 원, 오각형, 육각형, 칠교판, 쌓기나무) · 덧셈과 뺄셈 (two-digit

## 4. Written methods ("세로셈") and how they are laid out

Korean children write sums **"가로셈"** (in a line, 23 + 45 = 68) and **"세로셈"** (in columns, "세로로 계산하기").
Textbooks first show expanded/partial methods with **수 모형** (base-ten blocks: 백 모형, 십 모형, 일 모형) and only then
the short column form. One national method: there is no ABN-style alternative; the layout is close to the US/English
column method.

### Addition: "받아올림이 있는 덧셈"

```
     1  1        ← 받아올림 (written small above the next column)
     3  4  7
  +  2  8  5
  ──────────
     6  3  2
```
Said: "7 더하기 5는 12, 2를 쓰고 1을 십의 자리로 받아올려요. 4 더하기 8은 12, 받아올린 1을 더하면 13…". Result 632.
Terms: "더하는 수", "합". (1학년 1-2: 받아올림 있는 (몇)+(몇) with 10 만들기; 2-1: two-digit 세로셈; 3-1: three-digit.)

### Subtraction: "받아내림이 있는 뺄셈" (decomposition)

When the top digit is too small, **one ten is taken from the next column**: the next digit is crossed out and
reduced by 1, and **10 is written above** the column that needed it.
```
     4 12 12     ← 백: 5→4 · 십: 3→2, +10 = 12 · 일: 2 + 10 = 12 (often shown as a small "10")
     5  3  2
  −  1  7  8
  ──────────
     3  5  4
```
532 − 178 = 354: 12 − 8 = 4; 12 − 7 = 5; 4 − 1 = 3. Check: 354 + 178 = 532. Exact small-numeral placement varies by
textbook (UNCERTAIN); keep diagrams simple: cross out, write the new digit above and a small "10".
Terms: "빼지는 수", "빼는 수", "차". (1-2: (십몇)−(몇); 2-1: two-digit; 3-1: three-digit.)

### Multiplication

```
        2  3  4
     ×     2  3
     ──────────
        7  0  2     ← 234 × 3
     4  6  8  0     ← 234 × 20 (textbooks write the 0 or leave it out once children are confident)
     ──────────
     5  3  8  2
```
Terms: "곱하는 수", "곱". Times tables are read in Sino-Korean: "칠 팔 오십육" (7 × 8 = 56). 3학년: (두 자리)×(한 자리)
first as (몇십)×(몇) and partial products with 수 모형.

### Division: long division with the bracket ("나눗셈 세로셈")

Divisor on the **left** outside a bracket, dividend under a line, **quotient (몫) on top**, subtractions written
underneath, remainder (나머지) at the bottom. This is the same shape as English/US long division (not the "bus stop"
short form):
```
          2  1  4
       ┌─────────
    4  )  8  5  7
          8
         ─────
             5
             4
         ─────
             1  7
             1  6
         ────────
                1
```
Written in a line: **857 ÷ 4 = 214 … 1** (read "몫은 214, 나머지는 1"). Check (검산): **4 × 214 = 856, 856 + 1 = 857**,
i.e. 나누는 수 × 몫 + 나머지 = 나누어지는 수. Terms: "나누어지는 수", "나누는 수", "몫", "나머지", "나누어떨어진다"
(remainder 0). The curriculum asks that 검산 focus on why checking matters, not on rote. England's grid method, chunking
and bus-stop layout are not used in Korea: don't show them.

## 5. Notation

- **Decimal point**: a point, "소수점": 2.5 (read "이 점 오"), 0.25 ("영 점 이오"). Never a comma.
- **Thousands**: a **comma every three digits** in everyday writing and prices: 1,000원, 25,000원, 1,250,000. **But
  numbers are read in groups of four**: 만 (10⁴), 억 (10⁸), 조 (10¹²). 12,345,678 = "천이백삼십사만 오천육백칠십팔",
  and the 큰 수 unit writes "1234만 5678". So "a million" = 백만, "a billion" = 십억. Textbooks of the 큰 수 unit often
  write big numbers with no comma and teach the 만/억 grouping (UNCERTAIN how each book prints them). Recommended for our
  pages: commas in money and everyday numbers; when teaching big numbers, show the 만/억 grouping as well. Never a comma
  in years (2026) or times.
- **Multiplication**: **×** (read "곱하기"); **division: ÷** (read "나누기"), both in primary. "·" and "/" are not used
  in primary. Remainder in a line: "17 ÷ 5 = 3 … 2" (the "…" sign).
- **Equals**: "=" read "는/은" ("3 더하기 4는 7"). **Comparison**: >, < ("~보다 크다/작다"), 1~2학년.
- **Unknown**: a box □ (2학년), then □ and △ (5~6학년); no letters.
- **Fractions**: always stacked; read **denominator first**: ¾ = "**4분의 3**", ½ = "2분의 1" (also "반"). Mixed number:
  "1¾" read "**1과 4분의 3**" (대분수). 가분수 = improper fraction. Fraction characters (½ ¾ ⅓) are fine in HTML; in
  running Korean text "4분의 3" is natural.
- **Percent**: "25%" (no space), read "25퍼센트"; 백분율 is the school word. **Ratio**: "3 : 2" read "3 대 2".
- **Ordinals**: 첫째, 둘째, 셋째… (counting order in 1학년; said, but 1~2학년 children are not asked to write them in
  Hangul); "1번째" is also seen.
- **Units**: metric only, written after the number: 3 cm, 2 m 40 cm, 5 mm, 1 km; 1 L, 500 mL (capital L in textbooks);
  g, kg, t; ° (도); cm², m², km²; cm³, m³. Spoken "센티미터", "미터", "리터", "밀리리터", "그램", "킬로그램", "톤".
- **Money**: "1,500원" (원 after the number, no space); read "천오백 원". The sign ₩ is rare on price tags; write 원.
- **Times**: "3시 15분" in words; digital "3:15"; 24-hour "15:15" on timetables. See section 7.
- **Dates**: "2026년 10월 10일 (토)"; numeric "2026. 10. 10." Year–month–day order.

## 7. Time

- Korean distinguishes **시각** (a point in time: "지금 시각은 3시 15분") and **시간** (a duration: "1시간 30분 걸렸어요").
  Textbooks teach both words from 2학년 ("시각과 시간"): use them correctly.
- Hours are said with **native Korean numbers**, minutes with **Sino-Korean**: 3:00 "**세 시**", 3:15 "**세 시 십오 분**"
  (3시 15분), 3:30 "**세 시 삼십 분**" or "**세 시 반**" (3시 30분 / 3시 반), 3:45 "**세 시 사십오 분**" or "**네 시 십오 분
  전**" (4시 15분 전). There is no "quarter past/to". Textbooks teach "몇 시 몇 분 전" with simple cases (5분 전, 10분 전),
  so "4시 10분 전" is the textbook style; "15분 전" is everyday speech.
- **12-hour clock with 오전/오후** in everyday speech ("오후 3시"); 정오 (12:00 noon), 자정 (midnight); 2학년 learns
  "1일 = 24시간" and 오전·오후. **24-hour times** appear on timetables (KTX, buses), TV listings and phones; they are **not in the 2022
  curriculum text** (checked: [2수03-07]~[2수03-09], [4수03-13]~[4수03-14] cover 몇 시 몇 분, 1시간 = 60분, 1분 = 60초, 1일,
  1주일, 1개월, 1년 only; 오전·오후 and "1일 = 24시간" come from the 2-2 textbook), so introduce them gently as everyday
  reading (15:00 = 오후 3시), never as a school requirement. Whether any textbook shows a 24-hour time is UNCERTAIN.
- Daily rhythm for examples: school from about 8:40–9:00; 1학년 often finishes around 12:40–13:30 after 급식 (lunch),
  older years around 14:30–15:00 (UNCERTAIN, varies); 40-minute lessons with 10-minute breaks (쉬는 시간); then 방과후
  수업, 돌봄교실, 학원 (태권도, 피아노, 수영); dinner about 18:00–19:00.
- Dates: year–month–day. Wall calendars in Korea usually start on **일요일** (Sunday, in red) (UNCERTAIN as a rule; both
  exist).

## 9. Form of address and tone

- **Polite 해요체** throughout, as in our apps, store listings and `i18n/ko.json` ("무료로 체험할 수 있어요",

  never 반말 to adults.
- Korean drops "you": don't write "당신". Speak to the parent implicitly ("아이와 함께 해 보세요"); address them as
  "**부모님**" now and then (headings: "부모님께"); "보호자" is fine in formal or app contexts. The child is "**아이**"
  ("우리 아이" sparingly), "자녀" in formal sentences. Teachers: "**선생님**", "우리 반", "학생들", also in 해요체.
- **Indie** speaks friendly 해요체 with "나" ("나는 인디예요", "같이 풀어 봐요!"), as in the Kids app: she/her (in
  Korean simply "인디"; no pronoun needed).
- Warm, direct, plain: a 담임 선생님 talking to a parent at 상담. Avoid "쉬워요", "간단해요", "그냥"; avoid

  depends on the sound (write "8이 되는" carefully, or rephrase: "합이 10인 두 수").
- No emoji. Use proper symbols (×, ÷, −). Quotes: Korean uses ‘ ’ and “ ” (or 「」 rarely); the site uses ' and " in
