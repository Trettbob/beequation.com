About Beequation

Beequation is made by Trett Labs Limited, a small independent company in West Yorkshire, England. We make simple, well-made games and apps.

Why Beequation

Quick mental maths is a skill that helps every day, from checking change to reading a recipe. We wanted a puzzle that takes thirty seconds, works for a five-year-old and an expert alike, and feels good to solve. A honeycomb with one hidden number turned out to be just that.

What we believe

No ads. Nothing should interrupt a child, or anyone, in the middle of thinking.

No tracking. The apps collect no personal data. Progress, players and settings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Fair pricing. Both apps are free to try, with free levels and a daily puzzle. To open every level, subscribe monthly or yearly with a 7-day free trial, or buy Lifetime once. You can cancel any time.

Made for children properly. Beequation Kids is designed in line with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code: purchases, settings and links sit behind a grown-up check, and there are no streaks or timers to pressure children.
