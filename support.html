Support

Need help? Email hello@beequation.com. We usually reply within two working days.

I paid but the levels are still locked

Open Settings (Beequation) or the Grown-ups area (Beequation Kids) and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Can my family use one purchase?

Where Apple Family Sharing or Google Play Family Library is turned on, the unlock can be shared with your family group.

Is Beequation Kids safe for my child?

There are no ads, no chat and no data collection. Purchases, settings and the only links out (to our help and privacy pages) sit behind a grown-up check.

How do I reset progress?

Beequation Kids: Grown-ups, then Advanced, then Reset progress. Beequation: Settings, then Advanced, then Reset progress. This clears the progress of the player who is playing now; other players, the settings and your purchase stay. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app also clears it.

Can more than one person play on the same device?

Yes. Each app can hold up to six players, each with their own progress and settings. In Beequation, add players in Settings. In Beequation Kids, a grown-up adds them in the Grown-ups area, and children tap their own picture to play. Names stay on the device. One purchase unlocks the full game for every player.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (your order history), not by us.

How does the game work?

Every honeycomb hides one number that is the sum, or product, of two others. Tap it. Big Hive puzzles have you solve four small combs, then a comb made of their answers. Try it in your browser.
