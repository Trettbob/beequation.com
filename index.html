What is Beequation?

Beequation is a mental maths puzzle game made by Trett Labs Limited in the UK. Each puzzle is a small honeycomb of numbers, and exactly one of them is made from two others: by adding them, or in times-table puzzles, by multiplying them. You find that number and tap it. Puzzles start with sums to 10 for five-year-olds and go up to four-digit numbers, negatives, fractions, decimals, the three-number Trio and the Big Hive for adults.

There are two apps: Beequation Kids for ages 5 to 12, and Beequation for teens and adults, with a new Daily Pro puzzle every day. Both are free to try, have no ads and collect no personal data. Learn how to play or play free in your browser.
