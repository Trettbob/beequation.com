If your child's maths homework leaves you scratching your head, you're in good company. Methods have changed, the words are new, and it's been a while since most of us thought about long division. The good news is that the most helpful things you can do at home don't need any of that: a few minutes of maths talk a day, plenty of encouragement, and a calm "show me how you did it". These guides explain the rest in plain English.
