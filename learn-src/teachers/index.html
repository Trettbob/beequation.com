Practical, low-prep ideas for primary maths lessons, written by people who know how busy a school day is. Everything here follows the National Curriculum in England, works with the equipment most classrooms already have, and is free to use. Start with a five-minute starter tomorrow morning, or plan a whole lesson around reasoning.

Guides for teaching maths

Five-to-ten-minute warm-ups for KS1, lower KS2 and upper KS2, each with a worked example. Question stems, true or false, spot the mistake, bar models and sentence stems by year group. 15 low-prep games for the whole class, pairs and small groups, with ways to adapt them. Support and stretch in one lesson: manipulatives, scaffolds, challenge cards and early-finisher work.

Free resources for your class

24 printable Beequation puzzles, sums to 20 and times tables, with an answer key. Printable worksheets for every topic, each with an answer sheet. What pupils learn in maths in each year group, from Reception to Year 6. Short, regular practice of number facts pays off across every topic. Five minutes at the start of each lesson, revisiting facts from earlier in the year, keeps them fresh for when they're needed in fractions, measurement and problem solving.
